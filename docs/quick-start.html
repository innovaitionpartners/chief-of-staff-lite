Quick start · Claude & ChatGPT

Chief of Staff Lite.
A brief built around you.

Chief of Staff Lite turns your priorities and available work information into a focused daily brief: decisions to make, meetings to prepare for, and follow-ups to move forward.

Download the skill ZIP ↓

What makes it personal

During setup, you describe your responsibilities, priorities, key relationships, and where work gets stuck. The skill uses those answers to write a procedure for how it should investigate, weigh evidence, and prepare your brief.

For example, if meeting follow-ups are your bottleneck, it can identify unresolved commitments, reconcile them against available updates, and prepare the next ask. A leader focused on hiring would get different investigation and decision preparation.

Before your setup is finalized, the AI reviews that procedure for meaningful personalization, source limits, evidence handling, and the required brief format. You approve a summary of your setup.

Install once. Make it yours.

  1. Upload the starter skill

    Download chief-of-staff-lite.zip using the button above and keep it zipped. Choose your platform below. This is a standalone skill.

    Claude / Cowork

    1. Open Customize and select Skills.
    2. Click Add → Upload skill.
    3. Drop the skill ZIP into the upload area, or browse to select it.
    4. Complete the upload and confirm Chief of Staff Lite is available and enabled.

    ChatGPT

    1. Open Plugins in the sidebar, then select Skills.
    2. Click the plus icon.
    3. Choose Upload from your computer.
    4. Select the skill ZIP in the upload area and complete the upload. Confirm Chief of Staff Lite is available.

    “Plugins” is the sidebar menu name. You are uploading one skill through its Skills tab.

    Your account must allow custom skills, code execution, and file creation.

  2. Start the setup conversation

    Start a new chat or task in your chosen platform with the skill available, then use this prompt:

    Type this
    Set up my Chief of Staff Lite.

    Have your top three to five priorities in mind. You’ll discuss your role, decisions only you can make, important stakeholders, and preferred brief length. Rough answers are fine.

  3. Choose the information it can use

    Connect the email, calendar, task, and meeting-note sources you want included, then tell the assistant which accounts, projects, or channels matter. In ChatGPT, manage connections in Settings → Apps. In Claude, use Customize → Connectors. Finish sign-in and any required permissions. The skill does not connect accounts for you. Your first brief should confirm which sources it could read.

  4. Review and approve your setup

    The assistant posts a summary of your priorities, sources, preferences, and the assistance it will provide. Correct anything that’s off, then approve the setup. It creates chief-of-staff-lite-personalized.zip.

  5. Install the personalized version

    Download that new ZIP and return to your platform’s skill upload screen:

    • Claude / Cowork: Customize → Skills → Add → Upload skill.
    • ChatGPT: Plugins → Skills → plus icon → Upload from your computer.

    Keep the downloaded personalized ZIP and your setup summary before removing anything. If the uploader offers to replace the existing Chief of Staff Lite, accept that replacement. If it does not, remove the previous Chief of Staff Lite from your personal Skills list, then upload chief-of-staff-lite-personalized.zip. Confirm the personalized copy is enabled and the starter copy is gone. If the starter is managed by your organization and cannot be removed, ask your administrator to help replace or disable it.

    Make sure to do the second upload. Creating the personalized ZIP does not install it. It contains your business context, so keep it in your own account rather than publishing it as a shared team skill.

  6. Run your first brief

    Type this
    Run my daily CEO brief.

    The skill uses your saved procedure and available sources. It identifies missing coverage and can include up to two follow-up email drafts if you chose that option. You review and send them yourself.

Set it up to run every morning

Once your personalized skill is installed, run one brief and check that it uses your priorities and connected sources. Then schedule it. Replace the example time and timezone below with your own.

Type this
Every weekday at 8:00 AM America/New_York, run my daily CEO brief using my installed, personalized Chief of Staff Lite skill and its configured connected sources. Produce the full brief, not a reminder to run it. Identify any sources you cannot access. Keep follow-up drafts unsent. Name the task “Daily CEO brief.”

  2. Click New task → Create with Claude.
  3. Paste the scheduling prompt above, with your preferred time and timezone.
  4. Answer any scheduling questions. Review the proposed name, instructions, and schedule, then click Schedule.
  5. Open the task in Scheduled to review runs or change, pause, or delete it. Run it on demand to check the result.

If your Claude interface no longer separates Chat and Cowork, enter the prompt in a conversation, review the proposed task, and click Schedule. Claude scheduling instructions.

In ChatGPT Work

  1. Open Work where your personalized skill is available.
  2. Send the scheduling prompt above, with your preferred time and timezone. Review the task confirmation.
  3. Open Scheduled and select Daily CEO brief. Check the saved instructions, recurrence, and timezone. You can also create a task from Scheduled.
  4. Set delivery alerts in Settings → Notifications: Push, Email, or both.
  5. Use Scheduled to review results or edit, pause, or delete the task. If Scheduled is absent in your desktop app, use ChatGPT on the web.

If you cannot find the task list, use Settings → Notifications → Manage tasks. ChatGPT scheduling instructions.

Check the first scheduled result: it should contain your brief, use your personalized priorities, and show which sources were checked. If you get a reminder instead, edit the saved instructions to ask it to produce the brief. If the skill or sources are unavailable in that run, resolve access before relying on the schedule.

What you’ll get

Your brief brings together the decisions, meetings, risks, and follow-ups that matter to your work. Each daily brief has these seven sections:

Substantive items identify their sources and distinguish facts from inferences. When evidence conflicts, the skill considers what each source actually establishes; a newer message does not automatically settle the issue.

  1. 01
    Today in one sentence

    The most consequential focus for your day.

  2. 02
    CEO attention required

    Decisions and unblockers that need you.

  3. 03
    Meetings to win

    Useful preparation, outcomes, and questions.

  4. 04
    Risks and surprises

    Signals that could change your plans.

  5. 05
    Follow-through

    Open commitments and the next ask.

  6. 06
    Protect the agenda

    What to delegate, defer, or keep off your plate.

  7. 07
    Coverage gaps

    What could not be checked or established.

Optional follow-up email drafts

If you enable drafts during setup, the brief can include up to two emails after its seven sections—for example, requesting an overdue update, confirming a meeting commitment, or asking someone for a decision. Drafts appear when there’s a useful follow-up to prepare. You review, edit, and send them; nothing is sent automatically.

You can also give the AI permission to learn your email style from your 20 most recent Sent emails. It reviews only your own writing, not received messages, and keeps three representative excerpts in your private personalized skill after removing signatures, quoted history, email addresses, recipient metadata, legal footers, and irrelevant confidential details. This review is optional; you can still get drafts without it.

When your priorities change

Type this
Update my setup. My top priority is now…

It revises the affected parts without repeating the whole interview. Approve the updated summary, download the new personalized ZIP, and replace the previous skill using the steps above. Check your scheduled task afterward so its instructions still match what you want.
